### 3. CTA Analysis

Top performer CTAs are **action-transactional** ("Shop Now", "Buy Now") that create forward momentum. They match the urgency of the headline — the entire ad unit feels like a single coherent push toward conversion.

Bottom performer CTA ("Learn More") signals an informational intent mismatch — if a user was already browsing a sale, asking them to "learn more" breaks the conversion flow. It introduces a decision pause where there should be a decision close.

### 4. Three Actionable Recommendations

**Recommendation 1 — Rewrite Variant B headline structure**
Replace brand-name opening with a benefit + number formula:
- ❌ Current: "Brand Name — Quality You Can Trust"
- ✅ Revised: "Save ₹800 on Premium Products — Free Shipping Today"

**Recommendation 2 — Add urgency signal to every CTA**
Change "Learn More" → "Shop Now – Offer Ends Tonight" to align CTA with purchase intent signals already present in the audience targeting.

**Recommendation 3 — A/B test social proof in body copy**
Insert a quantified trust signal in the first line of body copy: *"Join 12,000+ customers who rated us 4.8/5"* before the product description. This reduces hesitation for cold audiences.
